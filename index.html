OUR INTRODUCTION

Understanding the Power of Spirituality

Brahma Kumaris is a spiritual organization that teaches meditation, self-awareness and positive living.

Its teachings focus on understanding the soul, connecting with the Supreme Soul and transforming our thoughts and actions.

Rajyoga meditation is one of its central spiritual practices.

OUR ROOTS

History of Brahma Kumaris

Learn about the beginnings of this spiritual movement and its development.

1936

The Beginning

The organization traces its beginnings to 1936 in Hyderabad, Sindh, then in British India, under the spiritual leadership of Dada Lekhraj, later known as Brahma Baba.

It initially became known as Om Mandali and later developed into the Brahma Kumaris organization.
